# Callings and Crowns

Back to the [overview](PROJECT.md).

---

## The idea

**In one sentence:** a calling is a way of life you can take up anywhere, and it pays you in Crowns.

There is no government, no guild, no boss who hires you.
A calling is simply something you are drawn to do: fishing, mapping, hunting, digging, plundering.
You do it, you get paid, you move on.

Callings follow the same design rule as the rest of the game:
**calm and intense, side by side.** Some callings are a quiet evening by the river.
Others are a fight for your life in an arena. You pick the mood.

### How it works

- You take up a calling from the same menu as your class tree and stats,
  **or** by talking to an NPC at the fixed hub.
- Every calling has a **cooldown** after you finish it. You cannot repeat the same one right away,
  but you can do a different one in the meantime.
- Callings are **separate from your class and group**. A Knight can be a Fisherman.
  A Wizard can be a Miner. Nobody stops you.
- Some callings are open from the start. Others open once you have achieved something
  in the world that fits the calling.

---

## The currency: Crowns

*A proposal, not a final decision.*

**Crowns** are old coins from the sunken kingdom. Nobody mints new ones anymore,
but they are everywhere: in ruins, with traders, in the pockets of whoever survived.

Because nobody knows anymore who the new king should be, everyone still accepts them.
One coin, usable everywhere, at every trader.

The coin is literally a relic of what drowned.

## The 12 callings

Four kinds, three callings each. From calmest to most intense.

### Calm - exploration

For when you want to wander, look around, and breathe.

| Calling | What you do | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Cartographer** | Maps unknown land. Rewarded for finding new places, not for danger. | From the start |
| **Herbalist** | Gathers rare plants, mushrooms and herbs in the wild. Slow, quiet, always something to find. | From the start |
| **Ruin Diver** | Searches old ruins for relics from before the fall. | Once you have your first iron ingot. The moment you realise there is more under the ground than stone |

### Steady - honest work

For when you want something reliable. Low risk, steady pay.

| Calling | What you do | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Miner** | Digs ore out of the ground, in bulk. | From the start |
| **Fisherman** | Fishes at the coast and in rivers. Now and then something unusual bites. | From the start |
| **Woodcutter** | Chops wood and delivers it. The most honest work there is. | From the start |

### Heavy - combat

For when you want a fight.

| Calling | What you do | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Monster Hunter** | Hunts dangerous creatures marked as a threat. | Once you carry your first real weapon: an iron sword |
| **Bounty Hunter** | Hunts a named target. Not a random monster, but someone who did something. | After a handful of Monster Hunter contracts. You have proven you can handle it |
| **Pit Fighter** | Survives wave after wave in an arena, for a crowd and for coin. | Once you wear a full set of iron armour. The arena will not let you in without it |

### Strange - magic and dark

For when you want something nobody else is doing.

| Calling | What you do | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Relic Scholar** | Identifies magical items and deciphers old texts. Knows things others do not. | After you find your first magical item. See open points |
| **Beast Tamer** | Captures rare wild animals and brings them in alive. Patience instead of violence. | Once you have tamed an animal yourself |
| **Grave Robber** | Plunders graves and crypts. Profitable, risky, and not everyone approves. | Once you have entered a crypt or dungeon and made it out alive |

## The calling tree - 12 nodes

**In one sentence:** every calling has a small tree that makes it pay more, come back sooner, and go deeper.

No four branches, no Mastery like classes have. Deliberately lighter:
a calling is not who you are, it is what you do.

Only three kinds of nodes:

| Node | What it does |
|---|---|
| **More Crowns** | Every completion pays more than before |
| **Shorter cooldown** | You can do it again sooner |
| **Heavier versions** | Opens a harder, bigger version of this calling |

### Heavier versions

Every calling has heavier versions next to its normal form.
They do **not** open through progress in the world.
They open **only** through nodes in that calling's own tree.

**Fisherman:** a normal catch comes from a river.
The heavy version is a catch far out at sea. More dangerous, many more Crowns.

**Grave Robber:** first a single grave. Then a whole crypt.
The last node opens something that did not even exist when you started.

Simple to start. Deeper the more you put in. Never a second class system.

## What the quest mod can do for this

### The cooldown: works

Confirmed: the quest mod has repeatable quests with a **cooldown per quest**.
It starts once you claim the reward. No extra mod needed.

Because every player is their own party of one, everyone has their own cooldown.

### More Crowns and shorter cooldown: needs a workaround

The quest mod **cannot make** a single quest's reward or cooldown grow. Both are fixed.

Solution: each calling has **separate versions per tier**, and a node opens the better one.

| Tier | Reward | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|
| Miner I | few Crowns | long |
| Miner II | more Crowns | shorter |
| Miner III | even more | even shorter |

The player only ever sees one: the best tier they have unlocked.

**Still to solve:** if Miner I and Miner II are both open, their cooldowns run separately,
and you could alternate between them to dodge the cooldown.
The old tier must close when the new one opens. How exactly has not been worked out yet.

## Open points

- **Relic Scholar clashes with the loot rule.** He opens when you find a magical item,
  but loot is random. In an unlucky world he never opens.
  Proposal: also let him open through something certain, like surviving your first dungeon.
- **Closing old tiers** when a better one opens. See above.
- **Crowns is a proposal**, not a decision yet.
