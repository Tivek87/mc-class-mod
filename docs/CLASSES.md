# Classes and skill trees

Back to the [overview](PROJECT.md).

---

## How choosing works

Two steps. First a **group**, then a **class** within that group.

- The group decides *where your power comes from*.
- The class decides *how you use that power*.

Six groups, four classes per group. 24 classes.
Plus one class that stands outside every group: **The Forsaken**.

### The start screen

You choose before you start playing. The first time you enter a world:

1. A welcome screen greets you by name. **Continue game** goes on, **Leave game** takes you back to the main menu.
2. **Choose your group.** A list of the six groups, with The Forsaken set apart below them.
   A panel next to the list explains the group under your cursor: its story, where its power
   comes from, why you would pick it, and each of its classes with their role. Click a group to open it.
3. **Choose your class.** A list of that group's classes, each card showing its role.
   The panel explains the class under your cursor: a line of story, its special, its strong and
   weak sides, why you would pick it, and what it starts with. Click to pick it, then
   **Start as this class**. **Back** returns to the groups.

- **Your class is not a cage.** It only decides your skill tree: how you grow and which powers you unlock.
  It never locks anything away. A Knight can still cast spells, a Wizard can still wear heavy armour,
  and anyone can use any weapon, potion or item from any group. The start screens say this in a gold box.
- **Every class is available**, The Forsaken included. Nothing is locked or hidden.
- **You can always see your class.** It is shown above your inventory. Hover over it to read what it is.
- While you choose, you are **out of the world**: nobody can see you, nothing can hit you,
  and you take no damage. Once you start, you are back in your normal game mode.
- Leave halfway? The next time you join, you choose again. Your choice is final once you start.
- When you start, a ceremony plays that is **completely different for every class**, built around
  what that class does. It always plays out in full on the spot where it started, even if you walk
  away. Players nearby see it too. Everything happens around you, never just in front of you.
  - **The first time** (when you pick your class): the big version, up to ten seconds, with your class
    name big on screen at the climax.
  - **Every respawn after dying**: a short animation of two and a half seconds. It is a different
    animation from the first time, but fits the same class.

## Choosing guide

What each group and class is for, and why you would pick it over another.
The specials describe what the class grows into through its skill tree.

### Groups

| Group | Power comes from | Pick if |
|---|---|---|
| **Warriors** | Your own body. Armour, weapons and endurance - no magic at all | You want to be in the middle of the fight and simply hit hard |
| **Rangers** | The wild. Range, animals, traps and knowing the land | You like fighting from a distance, exploring, or having a companion |
| **Rogues** | Stealth and cunning. Surprise, speed and dirty tricks | You want to strike first, stay unseen and never fight fair |
| **Mages** | Magic. Spells that hit hard from range, in a fragile body | You want big spells and can keep enemies away from you |
| **The Faithful** | Faith. Healing, holy light and power against the undead | You want to protect, heal, or hunt what should not exist |
| **Alchemists** | Potions and powders. Healing brews, poison, bombs and crafting | You like preparing, brewing and winning with the right mix |
| **The Forsaken** | A little of everything: fragments of all six groups | You want no fixed role and the hardest possible start |

### Classes

| Class | Role | Special | Strong | Weak | Pick if |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Knight** | Tank · melee | Draws enemies to himself, blocks with shield and heavy armour | The hardest class to kill | Slow, low damage | You want to survive anything and protect others |
| **Berserker** | Damage · melee | Rage: hits harder the lower his health is | Huge damage up close | Little defence, easy to push too far | You love risky, all-in fighting |
| **Halberdier** | Control · reach | Long reach: hits before enemies can hit back | Keeps enemies at a safe distance | Struggles once something gets close | You like controlled fighting and good positioning |
| **Duelist** | Evasion · counter | Dodges and parries, then a strong counter-attack | Very hard to hit one-on-one | Light armour, struggles against groups | You want skill and timing over raw strength |
| **Archer** | Damage · range | Precise bow shots from long range | The most damage from a distance | Weak up close | You want to kill things before they reach you |
| **Beastmaster** | Companion · support | Animal companions that fight and grow with him | Two fighters instead of one | Weaker alone when his animal falls | You want a loyal animal partner |
| **Trapper** | Control · traps | Traps and snares that slow, catch and hurt | Very strong when prepared, great for defence | Weak when taken by surprise | You like planning ahead and luring enemies in |
| **Scout** | Speed · exploring | Speed, sharp sight, spotting danger early | The fastest explorer, finds things first | Low damage in a straight fight | You love exploring and moving fast |
| **Assassin** | Burst · stealth | Sneak attacks from hiding with massive damage | Kills a single target very fast | Fragile if the first strike fails | You want to strike once, hard, from the shadows |
| **Thief** | Loot · utility | Lockpicking and stealing: more loot, fewer fights | Gets rich fast, avoids danger | The weakest rogue in combat | You care more about treasure than fighting |
| **Highwayman** | Ambush · crossbow | Ambushes with crossbow shots and surprise attacks | A deadly first strike from range | Weak in a long, open fight | You like ambushes and a crossbow |
| **Infiltrator** | Stealth · access | Sneaks past guards into places others cannot reach | Unseen, reaches secret places | Not built for fighting | You want to sneak instead of fight |
| **Wizard** | Versatile · spells | Many kinds of spells: fire, ice, light and more | An answer for every situation | Fragile, master of none | You want the most options |
| **Sorcerer** | Raw power · spells | Fewer spells than a Wizard, but much stronger ones | The strongest single spells | Few options, needs rest to recover | You want pure, raw magic power |
| **Warlock** | Dark power · risk | Strong dark spells that always come with a price | Great power early on | Every power has a cost | You like risk and dark power |
| **Necromancer** | Summons · curses | Raises the dead, curses enemies, grows with every kill | An army of minions, stronger in long fights | Weak alone and at the start | You want an army of the dead |
| **Cleric** | Healer · support | Healing and blessings for himself and others | The best at keeping everyone alive | Low damage | You want to keep others standing |
| **Paladin** | Holy tank · melee | Heavy armour plus holy power against evil | Tough, extra strong against undead | Slow, less flexible | You want to be a holy warrior |
| **Inquisitor** | Hunter · anti-magic | Extra strong against the undead and forbidden magic | Destroys undead and spellcasters | Only average against normal enemies | You want to hunt the undead and the dark |
| **Monk** | Unarmed · agile | Fists and speed, stronger without gear | Fast, calm, needs no equipment | Little protection | You want a pure challenge without gear |
| **Apothecary** | Healer · potions | Brews healing and strengthening potions | Keeps himself and others alive | Needs time and ingredients | You like brewing and helping |
| **Plague Doctor** | Poison · weaken | Poison and sickness that wear enemies down | Brings down even strong enemies | Slow to kill | You like damage over time |
| **Bombardier** | Explosives · area | Bombs that hit many enemies at once | The best against groups | Dangerous up close, also to himself | You like big explosions |
| **Transmuter** | Crafting · supplies | Changes materials into more useful ones | More supplies than anyone | The weakest fighter | You like crafting and building more than fighting |
| **The Forsaken** | Everything · hard mode | Fragments of skills from every group | The most flexible, can grow into anything | Starts with nothing, no clear strength | You want the hardest start and total freedom |

## Warriors

*People of steel and sweat. No gods, no spirits, no tricks - just a body
trained to survive where others break.*

| Class | Who he is | What the sea left him |
|---|---|---|
| **Knight** | The wall. Not the fastest, not the cleverest, but the one still standing when everyone falls - and the one the monster looks at instead of you. | A chainmail shirt that is nearly rusted through, a blunt sword, three bread |
| **Berserker** | Fights with rage as fuel. The more he bleeds, the more dangerous he gets, until nobody - not even he - knows when he will stop. | An axe with a cracked handle, a torn cloak |
| **Halberdier** | Keeps his distance with a long pole and a sharp end. Calm, patient, deadly - the opposite of the Berserker beside him. | The shaft of his halberd. The blade is gone |
| **Duelist** | Elegance as a weapon. Wins not by hitting harder, but by never getting hit. And then, at exactly the right moment, he does. | His rapier, still whole but blunt. Nothing else |

|---|---|---|

| **Beastmaster** | Never fights alone. Wherever he goes, something goes beside him - loyal, wild, and exactly as dangerous as he needs it to be. | His dog. It survived too. Nothing else |

*People who have learned to bend the world itself. Arcane power, gained in four very

| Class | Who he is | What the sea left him |
|---|---|---|
| **Wizard** | Took it all from books. Knows a little of everything: fire, ice, light, and sometimes something darker, if the book allowed it. Broad, curious, never done learning. | A soaked spellbook. Exactly one page is still readable |
| **Sorcerer** | Was simply born with it. Fewer tricks than the Wizard, but what he does, he does with power nobody taught him. | A cracked pendant from his mother. Nothing magic about it - his magic is inside him, but he is drained and has to recover |
| **Warlock** | Borrowed power from something that is not human. It works, but there is always a price that only becomes clear later. | A broken seal. The voice behind it has been silent since the shipwreck |
| **Necromancer** | Learned to use death itself. Not just making skeletons walk - disease, curses, and power that grows with every enemy that falls. | A bone from someone who did not make it. One spell |

## The Faithful

*People whose power does not come from themselves, but from what they believe.
Strong for as long as their faith is.*

| Class | Who he is | What the sea left him |
|---|---|---|
| **Cleric** | Keeps others standing. Without him a hard fight literally falls apart, and everyone around him knows it. | A holy symbol on a chain. One blessing |
| **Paladin** | Wears the same armour as a Knight, but fights with something that is not steel. Straight at anything he sees as evil. | A dented breastplate. His sword is on the seabed |
| **Inquisitor** | Hunts what should not exist: the undead, forbidden magic, things that belong in the dark. | A crossbow without bolts and a list of names |
| **Monk** | Needs no weapon, needs no armour - only discipline. The calmest of everyone, and the most dangerous when it counts. | A string of prayer beads. Half of them were lost to the sea. He counts the ones that are left |

**Why the Paladin is here and not with the Warriors:** his power does not come from steel
but from faith. A group is about the source, not the weapon.

## The Forsaken - the class without a class

**No group. Stands outside the system, as a seventh path.**

This player does not wake up with scraps from the shipwreck. He wakes up with **nothing**.
Not a piece of a weapon, not a scrap of cloth, not a crumb of bread.

Worse: no memory of who he was before the water closed over him.
No group claims him, because he does not know himself which one he belongs to.

His tree is not a branch of one source. It is stitched together from fragments of all six groups.
Every node is an echo of a strange skill: a hint of a warrior's stance here,
a flicker of a spell there.

He is the living version of the idea that anyone can learn anything -
not by choice, but because he has no other path left.

**Difference from the Monk:** the Monk chooses to own almost nothing, and still kept his prayer beads.
The Forsaken never had a choice, and is the only one who wakes up with nothing at all.

**How you find him:** he is not secret. He is on the start screen like every other class,
on his own row below the six groups.

## Not a class - but you can still play it

Some fantasy roles are deliberately **not** built as a class.
That does **not** mean you cannot play them.

You have full freedom. Your class shapes where you start and how you grow,
but it never locks you out of anything. Gear, callings, animals, potions and the world
are open to everyone. So you can grow into almost any style yourself.

What you do not get is a class built around it: no dedicated skill tree, no Mastery,
no shipwreck scraps made for it. You build it from what the world offers.

### Battlemage
Half warrior, half mage. Not a class, because the Mages are all four pure spellcasters in
light armour, and a battlemage in heavy armour does not belong among them.

**How to play it anyway:** pick a Mage, then put on the heaviest armour you find and step
into melee. Nothing stops a Wizard in plate. Your tree will still be a Wizard's tree,
so you are a Wizard who fights up close - which is exactly what a battlemage is.

### Druid
Nature magic. Not a class, because it sits half with the Rangers (nature) and
half with the Mages (magic), and fits neither.

**How to play it anyway:** pick a Beastmaster and let animals fight at your side,
take up the Herbalist and Beast Tamer callings, and live off the wild.
Or pick an Apothecary and brew from what grows. You will not shapeshift,
but you will live like a druid.

### Any other idea
The same goes for anything else you can think of. A pirate, a hermit, a bard, a hunter
of the undead. If the world lets you do it, you can be it. Nobody checks.

### Rule for later
Only add a class if it **logically belongs to its group**.
If it fits nowhere: make a new group, or do not add it.

The Forsaken is the only exception, and that is exactly his story.

## Skill trees

Every class gets its own tree. Not every group, every class.
So a Wizard and a Sorcerer grow in different directions.

Every tree has two parts:

> The main tree is growing into your role. Mastery is becoming the legend of your role.

### The main tree - 26 nodes

| Node | Count | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Awakening** | 1 | Free starting point. Your path begins here |
| **Upgrade nodes** | 24 | Four branches of six |
| **Ascension** | 1 | Requires all four closers. Completes the main tree and opens Mastery |

Each branch of six:

| Part | Count | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Opener | 1 | Where the branch begins |
| Side path A | 2 | One direction |
| Side path B | 2 | The other direction |
| Closer | 1 | Finishes the branch |

**The side paths exclude each other.** Pick A, and B is locked for good.
So per branch you get four of the six nodes.

How much you can reach in the main tree in total:

| | Count |
|---|---|
| Awakening | 1 |
| Four branches, four nodes each | 16 |
| Ascension | 1 |
| **Total reachable** | **18 of 26** |

That is why two players with the same class still play differently.
And why a second playthrough is worth it.

### Mastery - 8 nodes

A separate mini-tree. Opens only after Ascension.

| Node | Count | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Mastery start** | 1 | Free once Ascension is reached |
| **Mastery nodes** | 6 | Mega upgrades |
| **Transcendence** | 1 | The absolute pinnacle of that class. Requires most of the six before it, for example four of the six |

Mastery nodes are **not small stat bonuses**. These are powers you cannot find anywhere
else in the world, not even by accident in a chest.

### What a main tree node can contain

- **Abilities and spells** - a new spell, a lunge, a shield bash, a trap
  (the first seven spells are already in the game, open to every class - see [Spells](SPELLS.md))
- **Weapons and armour** - the recipe to make something, not the thing itself
- **Upgrades** - more health, faster recovery, more mana,
  higher critical hit chance, more protection, faster movement, stronger spells

Not every class gets the same kinds. A Wizard needs mana, a Knight needs protection.

### Why the tree matters more than loot

Loot is random, so it is luck. One world hands you something good early, another only late.

> The skill tree is the reliable source of power. Loot is the bonus.

That is why weapon and armour nodes give **recipes**, not items.
A recipe always works. Finding an item is luck.
